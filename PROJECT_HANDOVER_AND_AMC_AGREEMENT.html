1. Executive Project Summary

MeroDokan Saloon & Spa POS is a high-performance desktop application engineered specifically for beauty salons, hair studios, luxury spas, and cosmetic parlours. The platform delivers 100% offline operational independence, eliminating counter lag and ensuring uninterrupted billing even during internet outages. The software manages dual-item invoices (services and retail goods), calculates individual stylist commissions automatically, schedules client appointments without double-booking, audits product stock movements, and reconciles cash at day-end.

★ Core Architectural Highlights:
• Zero Internet Dependency: Ultra-fast responsive UI built on C# Windows Forms and local MS SQL database.
• Automated Multi-Tier Commissions: Line-item level commission tracking for service therapists and retail sales staff.
• Dynamic Clash-Free Appointments: Visual booking board with real-time stylist availability locking.
• Dual ESC/POS Thermal Printing: Native high-speed formatting for 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) receipt rolls.
• Local Data Sovereignty: Complete data security with encrypted automated multi-destination database backups.

2. Complete End-to-End Module Workflows

1 Smart Express POS & Multi-Tender Checkout

Process Flow: Select/Search Customer (or Walk-in) $\rightarrow$ Add Services (Hair, Skin, Spa) + Scan Retail Products $\rightarrow$ Assign Stylist per line item $\rightarrow$ Apply Line or Overall Discount $\rightarrow$ Select Payment Mode (Cash, UPI/QR, Card, Split, or Customer Due) $\rightarrow$ 1-Click Save & Thermal Print with automatic cash drawer trigger.

2 Visual Appointment Calendar & 1-Click Bill Conversion

Process Flow: Create Booking $\rightarrow$ System verifies stylist availability to eliminate double-booking $\rightarrow$ Monitor lifecycle on visual timeline (Scheduled $\rightarrow$ In-Progress $\rightarrow$ Completed) $\rightarrow$ Click Bill Now to auto-populate cart and checkout instantly.

3 Staff Roster, Attendance & Commission Calculation

Process Flow: Configure staff master & default commission % $\rightarrow$ Staff daily check-in / check-out attendance $\rightarrow$ Real-time commission credit on each bill item $\rightarrow$ Generate monthly commission summaries for instant payroll disbursement.

4 Customer CRM, Outstanding Dues & Loyalty Rewards

Process Flow: Central customer database with birthday/anniversary reminders $\rightarrow$ Earn loyalty points on billings $\rightarrow$ Redeem points during checkout $\rightarrow$ Track customer credit in Due Ledger with balance settlement receipts.

5 Inventory, Inward Purchases & Daily Stock Ledger

Process Flow: Purchase entry with supplier invoice & batch details $\rightarrow$ Automatic stock increment $\rightarrow$ Outflow tracking for Retail Sales vs. In-Salon Back-Bar consumption $\rightarrow$ Real-time Daily Stock Ledger Book audit.

6 Day-End Cash Register Settlement (EOD Reconciliation)

Process Flow: Record Morning Float $\rightarrow$ System auto-calculates Cash Sales + Digital Collections - Expenses $\rightarrow$ Cashier enters physical cash denomination count $\rightarrow$ System audits discrepancy (Over/Short) $\rightarrow$ Print official EOD summary handover slip.

7 Business Intelligence & Statutory Reporting (20+ Reports)

Process Flow: Filter by date, staff, category, or payment mode $\rightarrow$ Real-time computation of Sales, Stylist Commissions, Stock Valuation, Profit Estimates, and GST/VAT B2C summaries $\rightarrow$ 1-Click Export to Excel, CSV, and PDF.
